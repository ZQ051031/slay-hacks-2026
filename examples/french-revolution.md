# The French Revolution

- **Estates-General** met in May 1789 to discuss France's financial crisis.
- **National Assembly** was formed by deputies from the Third Estate in June 1789.
- **Tennis Court Oath** was a pledge not to disband until France had a constitution.
- **Bastille** was a prison in Paris stormed on July 14, 1789.
- **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen** was adopted in August 1789.
- **French First Republic** was proclaimed after the monarchy was abolished in 1792.
