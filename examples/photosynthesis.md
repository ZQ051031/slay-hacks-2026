# Photosynthesis

- **Chlorophyll** absorbs light energy in plant cells.
- **Chloroplasts** are the structures in plant cells where photosynthesis takes place.
- **Carbon dioxide** enters most leaves through small pores called stomata.
- **Water** is absorbed by the roots and moves to the leaves.
- **Glucose** stores some of the energy captured during photosynthesis.
- **Oxygen** is released as a by-product when water is split.
